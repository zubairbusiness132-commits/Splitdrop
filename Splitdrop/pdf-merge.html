PDF Merge

Combine multiple PDF files into one. Drag to reorder before merging.

How it works

1. Add two or more PDF files. 2. Drag rows to set the final page order. 3. Click Merge & Download to get one combined PDF file. Merging happens locally using pdf-lib — your documents are never uploaded to a server.
